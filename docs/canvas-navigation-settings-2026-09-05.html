Regression proof

  • first run restores stock wheel pan with standard Ctrl/Cmd zoom gain and no app preference
  • a plain scroll changes page position but leaves the scale unchanged
  • Ctrl/Cmd + scroll changes the stock camera scale
  • Canvas says Direct wheel zoom is off and keeps direct-only controls out of the way
  • enabling Direct wheel zoom switches the live stock camera and persists the opt-in
  • with direct mode enabled, a plain scroll-down zooms in
  • turning direct mode back off restores pan, hides its tuning controls, and resets modifier zoom to stock gain
  • stock navigation survives a full reload after opting out of direct zoom
  • the canvas navigation journey emits no local console errors

The implementation uses tldraw’s supported camera.wheelBehavior, setCameraOptions, and public inputMode preference. It installs no replacement camera or custom wheel-event handler.
