The setting says the whole contract

The default does not make people infer the modifier: it spells out both stock gestures directly, then removes direct-only controls until direct zoom is deliberately enabled.

CAD convention remains opt-in

Direct mode reveals its direction and sensitivity choices, plus a separate Ctrl/Cmd inversion switch. Turning Direct mode off restores stock panning and ordinary modifier zoom.

Regression proof

  • first run restores stock wheel pan with standard Ctrl/Cmd zoom gain and no app preference
  • a plain scroll changes page position but leaves the scale unchanged
  • Ctrl/Cmd + scroll changes the stock camera scale
  • Canvas says Direct wheel zoom is off and keeps direct-only controls out of the way
  • enabling Direct wheel zoom switches the live stock camera and reveals the optional modifier inversion
  • Canvas can opt Ctrl/Cmd + scroll into the opposite direct-zoom direction
  • with direct mode enabled, a plain scroll-down zooms in
  • with the option enabled, Ctrl/Cmd + scroll-down zooms out instead of panning
  • Ctrl/Cmd + scroll-up zooms in, opposite the direct wheel convention
  • turning direct mode back off restores pan, hides its tuning controls, and resets modifier zoom to stock gain
  • stock navigation survives a full reload after opting out of direct zoom
  • the canvas navigation journey emits no local console errors
