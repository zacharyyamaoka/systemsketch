The target is the one file nothing writes to

That is the whole point of the rename, and it is enforced in three places rather than asked for in prose:

# seeds a blank target only where none exists; never touches one that does
python3 -m pyblocks evaluate examples/systemsketch_goldens --seed-targets

# writes generated.systemsketch beside each target, and evidence into artifacts/
python3 -m pyblocks evaluate examples/systemsketch_goldens --write-artifacts

# refuses, by design, rather than replacing twelve hand-authored judgments
python3 scripts/bootstrap_systemsketch_goldens.py

A blank target is a blank board, not a parse error — which is what makes a freshly seeded case something you can click and start drawing in. The evaluator skips it until it has something in it, so an unauthored case is an empty case rather than a failing one.

5 · What was decided, and why

The extension ships a build, not the source

The obvious shortcut is to bundle src/ into a webview with esbuild. That would be a second build of the canvas, free to drift from the one you released and impossible to name afterwards. Instead stage_app.mjs runs the app's own vite build with --base ./ and stamps what it staged; tests/test_stock_boundary.py fails if the extension's bundler ever grows a webview entry point.

A worktree can never claim to be Stable

The release manifest records a source timestamp, and comparing timestamps across different checkouts is meaningless — a worktree checked out this morning is older than Stable by the clock and entirely different source. The gate compares the recorded sourceRoot as well, which is why this VSIX honestly reports development.

The board follows the workbench shipped

The host's light/dark choice now reaches tldraw itself — the journey reads .tl-theme__dark and the painted canvas colour off the webview — and the chrome around it wears the workbench's own --vscode-* variables through one theme block in src/theme/tokens.css. The inspector that used to render its title near-black on near-black is measured as a WCAG ratio in the host's theme, on the same element, and must clear 4.5:1. How the token layer works, and what it measured across five themes, is in the theming report.
