One command opens the whole review.

A retained runtime now serves the pinned app, guided board, lightweight report, and its ignored capture media together. The final chat handoff can stay to a title and one re-runnable command.

The retention boundary

→
First publishCopies ignored reports/media/<name>/ into the detached review worktree.
→
RestartServes the same page and retained captures after the implementation track is gone.

What changed

  • --report-media is constrained to the ignored media namespace and copied safely before a cold start.
  • An optional --report-builder runs in the pinned worktree with output and media paths supplied as environment variables.
  • The terminal uses OSC-8 links for short launch and view targets beside informative artifact titles.

What a handoff omits

  • No “Open it” table.
  • No file:// report link or percent-encoded board URL.
  • No runtime status, version, media, builder, or restart ceremony in the terminal card.
